## New findings discovered during re-review

1. **Blocker found and closed in the proposed contract: a witness could freeze forged reviewer findings.** Current v1 entries have a reviewer ID but no reviewer-authenticated signature (`eval/blind_review/README.md:89`; `eval/README.md:91`). A custodian could change `supported: false` to `true` or invent the named reviewer before a valid witness signs the file. `docs/design/matched-evaluation-contract-v3.md:43-54` now requires a domain-separated per-entry signature covering packet/projection digests, reviewer identity/time, scores, findings, and notes; independently SHA-pinned reviewer keys with algorithm/window and role separation; verification before control/witness and again at scorer; and exact signed-byte witness binding. `docs/change-requests/CR-0021-citation-support-fairness.md:21-32` and its ToDo carry forgery and key/role denials. Key possession does not prove display, organizational independence, trusted time, or honest semantic judgment; those stay external gates.
2. **Important found and closed in the proposed contract: source-exact packet citations may never have been emitted by the candidate.** Current control-review completed rows bind `raw_answer_sha256` and a raw-run artifact reference, but no native citation extraction or per-citation decision (`eval/schemas/control-review.schema.json:103-125`). Raw/packet answer equality and source-exact quote checks therefore allow a custodian to add a valid citation to an uncited answer. `docs/design/matched-evaluation-contract-v3.md:33-37` and [CR-0025](../change-requests/CR-0025-candidate-citation-provenance.md) now require an independently signed pre-distribution attestation over original candidate artifact, versioned faithful extraction, and the complete packet citation list. A document-level native reference maps only to that full approved document, not an evaluator-chosen pinpoint sentence. Final restricted control v3 embeds exact bounded preliminary bytes/signature, witness v4 freezes them, and scorer re-verifies without a new catalog path. A completed answer with an uncheckable citation remains completed and unsupported under a preregistered symmetric rule; deferral requires independent evidence of a preregistered evaluator/access/capture failure. Code, product-specific extraction profiles, and original-run truth remain open.

**Remaining proposed-design Blocker/Important:** None identified in this scoped re-review after the above revisions. The open implementation and external proof gates below are not design approval or observed evidence.

## Complexity and stage fit

The added signatures and extraction record protect two distinct claims: who judged citation support, and what the candidate actually cited. Reusing the existing independently pinned control signer and embedding bounded preliminary attestation bytes in final control review v3 avoids another signing service and a mutable scorer-catalog path. The proposed 32 KiB per-attestation, 128-packet, and 8 MiB final-control caps at `docs/design/matched-evaluation-contract-v3.md:37,99-101` need denial tests and may require a reviewed revision for a larger corpus. The evaluator should stop at a witnessed scorecard and human platform-selection gate; the full dashboard/broker build remains conditional on a measured incumbent gap.
