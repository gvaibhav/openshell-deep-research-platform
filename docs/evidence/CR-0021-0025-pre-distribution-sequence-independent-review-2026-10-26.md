## Sequencing and custody assessment

| Boundary | Accepted proposed rule and denial |
|---|---|
| **Approval and packet preparation** | `docs/design/matched-evaluation-contract-v3.md:38-40,48` requires approved raw corpus/source/rubric pins, matching canonical digests, a complete manifest v2, and exact packet ID/raw SHA-256 before any delivery. A self-computed pin, changed manifest, wrong packet, or mismatched raw/canonical input digest denies. The isolated `eval/target_projection.py:310-386` checks supplied byte pins and packet identity but deliberately does **not** verify the preliminary signature; its `preverified_*` arguments are not authority. |
| **Pre-review authority** | `docs/design/matched-evaluation-contract-v3.md:40` and `docs/change-requests/CR-0025-candidate-citation-provenance.md:20-22` require the independently pinned control signer to attest each completed cell's native citation extraction, exact packet ID/hash, complete manifest raw hash, and canonical input digests **before** packet or target delivery. The distributor verifies signature, manifest, approved raw bytes, FD-held packet, and `corroborated` status. A later witness cannot retroactively authorize a target already shown. |
| **Reviewer-edge byte and label identity** | An interim Important gap allowed verification of packet A followed by delivery of pathname-swapped packet B. The revised `docs/design/matched-evaluation-contract-v3.md:44-46` now requires one immutable verified FD-read byte buffer for both target derivation and packet delivery, no reopen or reserialization, and a reviewer-edge hash and packet-ID comparison **before display**. A switched or label-bearing B denies even when A's projection was valid. The candidate/run-labeled attestation, native artifact, and distributor log stay in restricted custody; only the verified anonymous packet and allowlisted target projection cross to the blind view. The red/green obligations appear in `docs/todos/CR-0021-citation-support-fairness.md:18-19` and `docs/todos/CR-0025-candidate-citation-provenance.md:16-18`. |
| **Later freeze and migration** | `docs/design/matched-evaluation-contract-v3.md:40,102-111` embeds the **exact** bounded preliminary bytes and detached signature inside restricted final control review v3; witness v4 freezes that file after blind reviews. The scorer re-verifies prelim, manifest/packet identities, candidate-run mapping, and cited spans. The path-only scorer catalog can remain v2 because no new catalog path is trusted. Missing or mixed versions, replaced equivalent-looking preliminary bytes, and late re-signing without independently retained delivery-time evidence deny observed readiness. |

**Examples:** Case-12's approved, source-exact full-record citation may be delivered with two reviewer-only targets after a valid preliminary signature and may earn support only after the blind reviewer signs both findings and later control/witness checks pass. A packet A whose path is switched to B after verification, a fake preliminary signature, a manifest whose raw bytes changed, a target shown before attestation, or a reviewer view containing candidate labels must be denied. A completed answer with an unverifiable native citation remains completed and unsupported under the preregistered symmetric rubric; the evaluator cannot defer it after seeing a weak answer.
