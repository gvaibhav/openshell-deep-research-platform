| Severity | Reproduced finding | Required correction |
|---|---|---|
| **Blocker** | `eval/scorecard.py` accepted `--output` equal to its approved `--corpus` path, exited successfully, and overwrote the corpus with a ready scorecard. | Exclude all consumed input/binding paths and aliases; publish only to a new, exclusive no-follow output path with atomic completion. |

| **Important** | Callable `build_scorecard` shallow-copied candidate runs. A deterministic hook changed a shared nested forbidden-source probe between aggregation and signed verification, yielding `binding: verified_local`, zero reported violations, and false readiness. This did not arise in the normal single-thread CLI JSON path without concurrent mutation. | Score and verify one immutable deep snapshot of the candidate objects. |
| **Important** | Re-signed one `status: deferred` row was rejected because blind raw observations and binding required every cell completed. The guide's verified-partial observed-violation behavior and unavailable-connector deferral path were unreachable. | Version a witness-frozen full candidate×case disposition contract: complete cells retain raw/packet/blind/control proof; deferred/failed cells have bounded reason and independently signed control evidence without fabricated answer/score. | **Important** | Individually finite `1e308` cost/latency/effort values aggregated to `inf`, yet the signed scorecard returned ready and CLI would emit nonstandard `Infinity` JSON. No external witness/key/time custody, real source/tenant event corroboration, approved corpus/rubric, observed ChatGPT or worker run, or human SEL decision was supplied.
